Dual Language Book

Listen to a book while you walk. The screen can stay off. A short passage in the original language, then the translation, then the original again.

How it works

The same passage, twice in the original — with meaning in between.

1

Hear the original

A short fragment in the language of the book.

2

Hear the translation

The same passage in the language you are learning from.

3

Hear it again

The original returns, now with the meaning already in mind.

Made for walking

Language learning that fits a walk.

Screen off

Keep listening with the phone in your pocket. Built for walking, not for staring at a page.

A ready library

Start with public-domain books, or add your own EPUB files.

Languages that fit real reading

Interface in English, Russian and Ukrainian. Books in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German and more. Translation into Russian, English or Ukrainian.
