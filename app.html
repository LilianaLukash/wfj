Read. Listen. Understand.

Dual Language Book

Improve your language without extra time.

Learn naturally by reading and listening to books in the original. Text, audio and translation sit in one loop, so a walk, a commute, a workout or an evening at home already counts as practice.

How it works

Original, translation and audio in one uninterrupted process.

1

Hear the original

A short fragment in the language of the book.

2

Hear the translation

The same passage in the language you are learning from.

3

Hear it again

The original returns, now with the meaning already in mind.

No extra hour in the day

Practice in time you already spend.

The screen can stay off. The book keeps going while you move through an ordinary day.

  • On a walk
  • On the road
  • During sport
  • At home

In one place

Text, audio and translation, without switching apps.

Original and translation

See and hear the same fragment in the language of the book, then in the language you already know.

Natural spoken voice

The book is read aloud in a clear voice, so you train your ear on real sentences, not isolated words.

Flexible listening

Choose the voice, the translation language and how often you hear the original again. Speed changes without rebuilding the book.

Listen offline

Download the next chapters to the phone and keep going without a signal — on a plane or a long walk.

What you get

A library you can start today, in the languages you actually read.

  • Free to start No extra lesson. Open a book and begin.
  • For A2–B2 Real books, not a course of invented phrases.
  • Text + audio + translation Original → translation → original, in one listening.
  • 19 original languages English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Polish and more.
  • Translation into 3 languages Ukrainian, English or Russian — whichever you think in.
  • A ready library Public-domain books to start with, or your own EPUB.
